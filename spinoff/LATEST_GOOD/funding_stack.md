## Can VLAIO and IOF stack? Only with deliberate budget separation.

**Important correction.** An earlier version of this doc said they "fund different things." That was too clean and under-sourced. The honest picture: **both instruments are predominantly personnel/salary funding**, so they cannot both pay *your* salary. They stack only if IOF funds a *different person* or non-salary costs.

What is actually well-sourced:
- **VLAIO Innovatiemandaat (spin-off track)** funds *you*: "personeelskosten van de mandataris (dus één werknemer)" + overhead/werkingskosten, with non-payroll capped at **~€50k/year** full-time (one VLAIO page still lists €40k, confirm). Spin-off phase **100% subsidised**, 0% equity. Mandate stops early on incorporation. So VLAIO = your salary. *(Source: VLAIO subsidiedatabank + Flanders.be.)*
- **IOF (UGent StarTT / ConcepTT):** StarTT ~€200-250k / 2 yr, ConcepTT ~€100-125k / 1.5 yr. **The cost breakdown is now confirmed** from a real funded StarTT in WAVES (F2024/IOF-StarTT/006, "EIoT Chip", `spinoff/F2024_IOF-StarTT_006...pdf`): of its €210k, **€150k (71%) was Personnel = the salary of one named post-doctoral researcher at 80% over 24 months**, plus €22k operating (compute/storage/licenses/travel), €36k subcontracting (an external hardware/dev firm via open tender), €2k equipment. So IOF StarTT is overwhelmingly **salary for one researcher**, with the **promotor (a professor/ZAP) unpaid**.

**So how does stacking actually work?** You cannot point two grants at one salary (yours). Two configurations only:

1. **IOF funds a different person.** The StarTT money hires a research engineer / postdoc / developer (not you) to execute the technical milestone, while VLAIO pays you. This genuinely stacks, and the upshot is **a teammate, not a second paycheck**. (This is exactly the EIoT Chip structure: promotor = unpaid professor, IOF €150k = a named hired postdoc. Clone that template, with Wout/a ZAP as promotor and a hire as the funded researcher.)
2. **They do not stack as salary.** If the intent is "IOF pays Robin," it collides with the VLAIO mandate (already paying you, min 50% FTE), so you pick one as your paycheck.

A hard structural reason it must be config 1: the VLAIO mandate is a near-full-time *personal appointment*. You cannot simultaneously be a full-time VLAIO mandataris and a full-time IOF-funded researcher. **In any world where you run both, IOF funds a hire (+ costs), not you.**

The "no parallel VLAIO channel" rule (mandataris "kan niet tegelijkertijd aanvrager zijn van een aanvraag binnen een ander steunkanaal van het agentschap") only bars another **VLAIO** instrument. IOF is a UGent fund, not a VLAIO channel, so it does not bar IOF.

**Worth doing both (ignoring workload)?** Conditional. "VLAIO + IOF = ~€250-335k stacked" is only real **if you are hiring someone**. IOF's true role in the stack is *money to fund your first employee*, not free cash on top of your own salary. For a software spin-off with ~€15-30k/yr non-salary burn, the non-salary slice of IOF is thin, so without a hire the marginal value of IOF-on-top-of-VLAIO is limited. **The strategic question is: do you want a salaried teammate (dev / research engineer) in year 1?** If yes, IOF funds them and the stack is excellent. If no, sequence them or use IOF for the smaller non-salary costs only.

**The exact question for Filip / iof@ugent.be:** "If I hold a VLAIO Innovatiemandaat paying my salary, can a parallel IOF StarTT fund a *separate hire* + project costs, with me or Wout as promoter, and which cost lines are eligible?" That turns this from logic into confirmed fact.

### Corrections to older docs
- IOF **StarTT goes up to €250k** (2 yr) and **ConcepTT up to €125k** (1.5 yr). The "50-125K" in `external_briefing.md` undercounted StarTT.
- VLAIO decision is **~3 months** after submission (Sept to Dec), not the "~6 months" in `external_briefing.md`.
- Use **StarTT / ConcepTT**, not Stepstone. Stepstone is more money and more strings than this ~15-30K/yr-burn business needs.

## How hard is it to get? (real numbers)

**VLAIO Innovatiemandaat** is a Flanders-wide competition but a *small, boutique* one. Most recent call:

> 25 IM applications submitted, **11 funded** (~€3M total). Of the 11: **7 spin-off mandates**, 1 Phase-1, 3 Phase-2.

So **~44% raw success rate**, and spin-off mandates were the *largest* funded category. The fall-2022 call had only 10 IM applications total. You are competing against ~20-40 people across all of Flanders, not hundreds. Much friendlier than it sounds.

**IOF (UGent)** publishes no acceptance rate, and I will not invent one. But it is structurally more gettable: it is an *internal UGent* budget allocation (you compete within UGent, not Flanders), and the **mandatory intake meeting pre-filters you** (IOF + TechTransfer + your ZAP decide the "optimal funding track" and flag "issues towards evaluation" before you formally submit). Realized odds conditional on submission are therefore higher. Ask iof@ugent.be for the real number.

## Timeline: VLAIO does NOT start a year after IOF

A point worth nailing because it is easy to get wrong. VLAIO start rule: 1st of a month, earliest the month after submission, latest 6 months after award.

| | Deadline | Decision | Start |
|---|---|---|---|
| IOF (June call) | 3 Aug 2026 | ~Sep 2026 | **15 Oct 2026** |
| VLAIO (Sept call) | ~Sep 2026 | Dec 2026 | **~Jan 2027** (latest Jun 2027; at-risk as early as Oct 2026) |

So the gap is ~3 months, up to ~8 in the worst case, and essentially **concurrent** if you start VLAIO at-risk (op risico van de wetenschappelijke promotor) in late 2026. Not a year. (The "12 months later" idea in `decisions.txt` is about *istart*, a separate lever.)

## How the process works

**VLAIO Innovatiemandaat (spin-off):**
1. Verkennend gesprek (online intake with VLAIO).
2. Meet UGent TechTransfer ≥3 weeks before deadline (kamila.mascart / karen.cure). Scored.
3. Submit online (spin-off has its own form), NL or EN. The oral defense is in the language you apply in.
4. Signed IP afsprakennota ~6 weeks after the deadline.
5. Admissibility check (formal criteria only).
6. **Oral defense:** pitch and defend before an expert panel, answer questions.
7. Decision by the board on the **motivated advice of external experts**. If budget is short, the highest-rated proposals win.

Two calls/year: March (decision July), September (decision December).

**IOF (StarTT/ConcepTT):**
1. **Mandatory intake** (ZAP + IOF + TechTransfer) before submission. Sets the funding track, flags evaluation risks.
2. Announce to iof@ugent.be, get the application form.
3. Submit by the deadline. Decision at the IOF-council.

Four submission dates per year.

### How VLAIO judges you (the grid)

Two dimensions x two targets = four scored buckets:

| | Scientific quality | Valorisation |
|---|---|---|
| **The candidate (you)** | strong | strong |
| **The project** | strong | the work |

- **Pre-won:** candidate-scientific (PhD, npj Wireless Tech, two BioEM prizes, deployed 54k-line platform) and candidate-valorisation (you built and shipped it).
- **Where the work is:** **project-valorisation.** The panel must believe there is a real market and a credible path to a Flanders spin-off. This is exactly where the customer-pull evidence from `company_outreach_and_ndas.md` (discovery notes, LOIs, the matched Sim4Life validation figure) earns its score. Get the published "evaluatierooster innovatiemandaat spin-off" grid from TechTransfer and write *to* it.

## What to do

1. **Book the IOF intake** (ZAP + IOF + TechTransfer) for July, ahead of the 3 Aug deadline. It is required and it is your best free read on whether you will be funded.
2. **Book the VLAIO verkennend gesprek and the TechTransfer meeting** for the September call. TechTransfer involvement is graded; you are already in their system (P2026/040).
3. **Line up an industrial mentor** for the VLAIO spin-off mandate (must be a company employee, guides on valorisation, company cannot claim IP). Candidates: Luc's Ericsson contact, a ZMT-orbit person, or a Belgian deep-tech entrepreneur/advisor. This is a real to-do, not a formality.
4. **Confirm the exact 2026B VLAIO date** (vlaams-federaal@ugent.be) and the IOF September date (iof@ugent.be).
5. **Get the evaluatierooster grid** and write the proposal to its four buckets.
6. **Feed the valorisation bucket** with discovery notes + any LOIs + the matched AEGIS-vs-Sim4Life validation figure. Same evidence that powers the customer meetings and the standards contribution.
7. **Confirm with Filip** whether VLAIO can start at-risk in Oct 2026 to align with IOF, and that the IOF and VLAIO budgets are cleanly separated (no double-funding).

**Bottom line:** for your profile VLAIO is *winnable* (~44% raw, spin-offs favored, candidate buckets pre-won) and IOF is *quite gettable* (internal, pre-filtered). The single biggest lever on both is the valorisation evidence, which is the same work the company meetings need anyway.
