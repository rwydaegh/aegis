The correction box in the flowchart collects the effects left out by
the geometric law. This section treats them one by one: curvature,
diffraction at the shadow boundary, and inter-body reflections. The
labels in \cref{fig:val-fdtd} (``Fresnel only,'' ``+
polarization,'' ``+ curvature \& diffraction,'' ``Full kernel,'' ``+
occlusion'') turn each correction on in turn against the same FDTD
reference.
