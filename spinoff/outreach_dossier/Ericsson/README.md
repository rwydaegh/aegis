## Lessons from the Grangeat call (drives everything below)

Robin's own notes in `../Nokia/resulting_meeting_notes.txt` say four things
that reshape how the Ericsson meeting should run:

1. **The 25-question tier bank was over-designed.** *"tbh it was tough for me
   to ask all questions back to back cuz its a conversation."* Meaning: fewer,
   framed for natural drop-in, not a rigid list. This dossier uses ~7 topics
   as scenes.
2. **Grangeat's own verdict was blunt:** *"more a research project than
   valorization. not in the agenda."* Do not enter the Ericsson meeting
   selling the same commercial thesis. Enter it selling technical validation
   + a research productivity multiplier for their evidence-generation work.
3. **The vendor-integration thesis is dead** — *"att will not buy nokia
   instead of ericsson cuz of this. no new customers. only if operators ask
   for this themselves."* Ericsson will likely say the same, but **it might
   not matter for this meeting** because Ericsson's evidence-generation team
   is more research-driven than Nokia's, and they have the Site Digital Twin
   product where AEGIS actually could slot in.
4. **The actionable standards path Grangeat gave** — 5-slide vote to the WG,
   Wout knows the operator delegates — is universal, not Nokia-specific. So
   no need to re-litigate the standards path with Colombi. Ask if he'd
   support a Wout-tabled contribution, not "how do I get in."

## Meeting goals — ranked and honest

Given the Grangeat reality-check, the honest ranking:

1. **Stanislav's technical read of AEGIS.** He is Ericsson's numerical-methods
   evaluator. If he thinks it's real, that's Ericsson Research validation,
   which is IOF gold and standards-committee currency. If he thinks it's
   research-not-product, that's honest info and better to know now than after
   a paper is published.
2. **Whether AEGIS could feed into Colombi's next evidence-generation study
   or into Ericsson Site Digital Twin.** These are the two real technical
   integration paths. Not a purchase order — a research or product
   collaboration. Ask for a small pilot / matched validation.
3. **Support letter for IOF.** Colombi is willing to talk (his email is
   warm), and a letter from Ericsson Research at Kista carries more IOF
   weight than a letter from Nokia's EMF Mitigation Lead saying "interesting
   research." Ask cleanly.
4. **Operator referrals.** Grangeat gave four already (Vodafone / Italian /
   Andrea / Manfred Rutner at A1). Colombi's team runs studies at operator
   sites (the AFL stadium was Optus network). More operator names.
5. **The digital twin question specifically for Bischoff.** He is on the
   IEEE conference paper "Digital Twin-Based EMF Compliance Assessment of
   Base Station Sites." That is exactly AEGIS's fit. If he engages, this is
   the single most productive scene of the meeting.
