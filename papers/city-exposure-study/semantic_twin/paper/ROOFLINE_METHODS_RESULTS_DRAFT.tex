\subsection{Co-located panorama, receiver, and body}
\label{sec:colocation}

The panorama camera, the reciprocal ray origin, the receiver position, and the
body coupling point share one position at every standpoint. The transmitter
does not. It lies on the declared roofline source support described in
\cref{sec:source-measure}. Co-location is what lets a street-level photograph
taken at the standpoint report on the surfaces the early interactions reach.

Standpoints are the points of an exact cached pedestrian street route rather
than a grid. The baseline route contract is
\texttt{registered\_span\_street\_v1}. Its two endpoints are the furthest pair
in the site's current admitted station report, and the route itself is the
exact waypoint-keyed cached walking response for that pair. Route preparation
never buys or writes a route during a run, so a missing or stale cache is a
preflight refusal and not a straight-line fallback.

An opt-in evidence route, \texttt{provider\_corridor\_v1}, restricts the walk
to connected provider graph paths whose distance to the nearest admitted
camera stays below 20\,m at every segment endpoint and at every exact Voronoi
breakpoint. It leaves the baseline route and its sealed bytes unchanged. The
verified 2026-08-07 snapshot selected endpoint spans of 49.005\,m at
Korenmarkt, 103.781\,m at Prague, 62.437\,m at Madrid, 52.395\,m at Mexico
City, and 72.160\,m at Tokyo Hachiko.

\subsection{Support mesh and material evidence}
\label{sec:atlas}

Geometry is a photogrammetric support mesh of the square, cropped to 250\,m.
The panoramas do not create that geometry. They register image evidence to it.
The collision hierarchy retains the original support faces, 617,091 at
Korenmarkt and 664,619 at Prague, because exact transport-mesh decimation has
no convergence evidence.

Material evidence is bound through a surface atlas. A dense semantic
segmentation names the object class, a promptable segmentation model is then
asked for the material inside the facade regions, and the fused result is
written to the atlas. The atlas material lookup is evaluated on the original
support mesh. The Blender display level of detail is visual only and is never
the transport mesh, so it changes no transport array and no transport time.

A triangle takes an image-derived material only when its interface posterior is
strictly above 0.5. An exact tie falls back to the declared geometric prior.
Regions the panoramas never observed keep that geometric prior, and their
coverage and fallback provenance are reported rather than hidden. A silent
wholesale fallback to the geometry-only mode is a preflight refusal.

Roughness is the finish-only model. Quadrature roughness is retained as a
historical sensitivity. The \texttt{masonry\_two\_level} and RCWA paths are
research models and produce no result reported here.

\subsection{Roofline source measure}
\label{sec:source-measure}

The source model separates two questions that are easy to conflate. How many
effective emitters belong to a site, and how those emitters are distributed
along the represented roofline.

The first is fixed by an areal density. With $\rho_A$ active sites per unit
ground area and a crop area $A_{\mathrm{crop}}$, the expected active-source
count is
\begin{equation}
  N_{\mathrm{site}} = \rho_A A_{\mathrm{crop}} .
  \label{eq:nsite}
\end{equation}
This is the physical scaling. It is not the number of numerical source
quadrature points, and it does not by itself place any source.

The second is the source measure. Sources are constructed from the observed
route-aligned roofline, and the retained roofline curve is the geometry the
route provides. Every quadrature point on that curve carries the arc length it
represents. For an edge running from $\mathbf{p}_i$ to $\mathbf{p}_{i+1}$ the
baseline element is the physical three-dimensional length,
\begin{equation}
  \ell_i^{(3)} = \lVert \mathbf{p}_{i+1} - \mathbf{p}_i \rVert_2 ,
  \qquad
  L_3 = \sum_i \ell_i^{(3)} ,
  \label{eq:arclength}
\end{equation}
and the normalized conditional source probabilities are
$p_i = \ell_i^{(3)} / L_3$, which sum to one. The exact Korenmarkt source
artifact holds 506 segments with $L_3 = 182.953912$\,m. The sealed Prague
artifact has $L_3 = 654.605$\,m.

Changing the measure leaves \eqref{eq:nsite} and the site-level scaling
untouched. It changes only the conditional placement of source mass, which is
why it can still move direct, specular, next-event, and body-coupled
contributions. Horizontal projected roofline length is retained as an explicit
opt-in sensitivity rather than a replacement. At Korenmarkt
$L_{xy} = 160.509814$\,m, a ratio $L_{xy}/L_3 = 0.877324$. In a paired seed-7
run at identical receiver positions, source-curve hash, transport
configuration, and point seeds, in which only the normalized weights differed,
projected weighting changed the mean pointwise raw transfer by $-0.89\%$
direct, $-3.13\%$ one-reflection specular, $-1.17\%$ diffuse, and $-1.41\%$
total. Mean pointwise peak $S_{ab}$ changed by $-1.23\%$, and mean $S_{ab}$,
absorbed power, and whole-body SAR each changed by $-0.56\%$. That is a
one-seed common-random-number comparison and not a convergence claim.

\subsection{Reference transfer and reported units}
\label{sec:normalization}

Two reference quantities keep network scale, scene transfer, and visibility
from being conflated. For receiver position $\x$ the unobstructed reference
transfer is
\begin{equation}
  D_{\mathrm{ref}}(\x) = \sum_i \frac{p_i}{r_i(\x)^2} ,
  \label{eq:dref}
\end{equation}
where the arc-length weights $p_i$ sum to one and no visibility test is
applied. The directional measure reported here is the raw next-event field
divided by $D_{\mathrm{ref}}$.

Results per unit active antenna density and per unit radiated power use
\begin{equation}
  S_0(\x) = \frac{A_{\mathrm{crop}} D_{\mathrm{ref}}(\x)}{4\pi} .
  \label{eq:s0}
\end{equation}
A physical scenario value follows by multiplying by
$\rho_A P_{\mathrm{EIRP}}$, so neither the site density nor the transmit power
ever has to be stated or defended. Every campaign output records
$D_{\mathrm{ref}}$, the visible direct transfer $D_{\mathrm{vis}}$, and the
multipath surplus separately.

\subsection{Transport contract}
\label{sec:transport}

The production transport contract is
\texttt{first\_material\_interaction\_v1}. For each source and receiver
direction it combines exactly three terms.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item[T1] The direct source-to-receiver term, computed exactly.
  \item[T2] The order-1 all-specular term, computed exactly.
  \item[T3] Stochastic next-event estimation of the first diffuse interaction,
  taken at the first blocking material vertex.
\end{enumerate}

The three classes are non-overlapping, so the campaign reports direct,
one-bounce specular, diffuse, and total transfer without double counting.

Deterministic specular candidates are found by adaptive refinement over the
source curve and the support faces. When the candidate set reaches 20,000, a
conservative mirrored-receiver triangle-cone broad phase is applied first. The
certified CUDA Float64 broad phase leaves the exact host final kernel
unchanged, uses about 96\,MiB of resident memory, and produced byte-identical
path arrays with zero lost candidates. The adaptive candidate budget is
normally 120 million. Tokyo uses a recorded 320 million cap because one
standpoint has no accepted order-1 path and therefore cannot satisfy a relative
convergence test around zero. Its 319.046 million candidate support is
enumerated in full rather than admitting an absolute convergence epsilon.
Candidate caps, broad-phase thresholds, and chunk sizes are computational
guards. They are not model parameters.

\subsection{A declared scope limit, no mixed suffix}
\label{sec:no-suffix}

\texttt{first\_material\_interaction\_v1} has no mixed suffix. A sampled path
stops at its first diffuse event and is not continued into a
diffuse-to-specular tail. Specular orders above one are absent. This is stated
as a scope limit rather than presented as a complete model, and the historical
\texttt{max\_bounces=3} hybrid is sensitivity evidence rather than evidence
that a three-bounce model was ever in production.

The limit is a decision with evidence behind it. A source-conditioned
conservative-screen mixed-suffix estimator was built, verified, and then
rejected. Its screen rejected only impossible finite image geometry and
nonpositive diffuse cosine, so it had no false negatives against the unchanged
suffix kernel, and an analytic test confirmed that its conditional-source
expectation equalled the exhaustive weighted source sum. The predeclared
promotion gates asked for at least a fourfold improvement in relative variance
multiplied by point wall time. Measured over 64 replicas at one problematic
standpoint in each of two campaigns, the relative variance-time gains were
0.7755 at Mexico City and 1.1716 at Tokyo, both below the required factor of
four. The pilot removed every zero score, from 0.703125 to 0 at Mexico City and
from 0.515625 to 0 at Tokyo, but conditioning the source alone shifted variance
instead of removing the dominant tail. Production code and configuration were
not changed.

\subsection{Body coupling}
\label{sec:body}

Body coupling uses the level-2 surface-field path with an anatomical phantom
and published tissue parameters~\cite{aegis, itis}. The three directional
measures of \cref{sec:transport} are coupled to the body separately, and their
per-triangle $S_{ab}$ fields are then summed. That preserves linearity and
removes a redundant fourth body solve. Direct and deterministic all-specular
body fields are cached once per route point across replicas.

Three endpoints are reduced from the summed field. The area-weighted body mean
is the absorbed power divided by the exact float64 total triangle area,
$p_{\mathrm{abs}} / A_{\mathrm{body}}$, in both the scalar and the batched
coupler. The reported peak is the peak of the replica-averaged triangle field
and not the average of the per-replica maxima. The third is whole-body specific
absorption rate.

The CUDA path uses an explicit DrJit Float64 reduction with fixed
512-direction blocks. A NumPy backend remains an explicit alternative, and the
selected backend is recorded in provenance. The CUDA body result is
deterministic and agreed with the reference to a maximum relative error of
$6.64\times10^{-16}$ in the verified benchmark.

\subsection{Sampling and numerical controls}
\label{sec:sampling}

Every campaign launches 200,000 independent and identically distributed primary
rays per standpoint and records 4,096 passive angular output cells. The cells
are output directions. They are not 4,096 independent launch strata, and no
result depends on reading them as such. Rotated Fibonacci launch is retained as
a sampling diagnostic and is not the adopted baseline, for the reason given in
\cref{sec:sampler-check}.

Replicas use seeds 7 through 22, and convergence is examined at looks 4, 8, 12,
and 16. Storage is bounded by scalar shards plus at most one full body field
per declared look and one current field, so a look-to-look comparison uses the
retained cumulative field rather than a retrace.

The 200,000-ray and 4,096-cell values are the production contract rather than
the result of a completed budget reduction. Candidate reductions in either
remain diagnostic until paired convergence checks cover scalar peaks,
whole-body SAR, and directional spectra under the current transport contract.

\subsection{Replica convergence}
\label{sec:convergence}

Total transfer is converged at 16 replicas at every site. The maximum change
from look 12 to look 16 is 0.0000819\,dB at Korenmarkt, 0.0001559\,dB at
Prague, 0.0004083\,dB at Madrid, 0.043625\,dB at Mexico City, and
0.019732\,dB at Tokyo Hachiko. Every value is below 0.1\,dB.

The two largest values belong to the two sites that carry shadowed standpoints
with no direct term, which is the subject of \cref{sec:zero-direct}. Mexico
City and Tokyo are not directly comparable with their historical 64-replica
runs, so no look-to-look number is carried across that boundary.

\subsection{Shadowed standpoints with no direct transport}
\label{sec:zero-direct}

Six standpoints have zero direct transport. They are points 0, 1, and 3 at
Mexico City and points 13, 14, and 15 at Tokyo Hachiko. Every watt in the
reported answer at those points arrives by reflection or by a diffuse event.

They stay in the sample. A standpoint whose direct term is exactly zero is a
shadowed pedestrian position rather than a failed calculation, and dropping it
would delete the part of the route where the square does the most to the
answer. Retaining them has one visible cost, described in
\cref{sec:topology}, and one computational cost, the enumerated 319.046 million
candidate support at the Tokyo standpoint that cannot satisfy a relative
convergence test around zero.

\subsection{A launch sampler check that did not change the baseline}
\label{sec:sampler-check}

The IID launch was tested against a rotated Fibonacci launch in a paired
two-city campaign under the historical hybrid contract. Both arms used the same
geometry, the same source identity, the same 16 seeds numbered 7 through 22,
and the same convergence looks. The comparison is retained here as a method
check on the sampler rather than as a current transport result.

The two samplers agree. At look 16 the Korenmarkt route median surplus was
1.12153\,dB for IID and 1.12156\,dB for Fibonacci, and the whole-body SAR
medians per unit $\rho_A P_{\mathrm{EIRP}}$ were 0.0631297 and
0.0631174\,m$^2$/kg. The largest paired difference in total raw transfer was
0.0324\,dB, and the largest paired difference across body mean, absorbed power,
and whole-body SAR was 0.0343\,dB. At Prague the route median surplus was
1.10519\,dB for IID and 1.10465\,dB for Fibonacci, with whole-body SAR medians
of 0.0121379 and 0.0121384\,m$^2$/kg.

Fibonacci does not earn adoption on variance either. The Korenmarkt
total-transfer p90 standard errors at look 16 were 0.00839\,dB for IID against
0.01051\,dB for Fibonacci, and the look 12 to look 16 p90 movement was
0.00298\,dB against 0.00350\,dB. The Korenmarkt total variance ratio relative to
IID had median 0.974 with q10 0.01395 and q90 15.21. Diffuse variance alone had
median 0.628, so the one component that improved did not carry the total. At
Prague the total variance-ratio q50 and q90 were 1.267 and 51.19, the diffuse
variance-ratio q50 and q90 were 1.153 and 3.437, and the body variance-ratio
median was 1.21171. The rotated Fibonacci launch is therefore kept as a
diagnostic and IID remains the production baseline.

\subsection{What the completed campaigns do not cover}
\label{sec:roofline-limits}

Five sites of an intended ten are complete, and the five carry one sampler,
one carrier, one crop, and one transport contract. No between-city ranking is
claimed from them.

Three limits are contract-level rather than statistical. Specular orders above
one are absent by declaration, as is any diffuse-to-specular suffix. The
reported convergence is Monte Carlo convergence over seed replicas, so it
describes estimator uncertainty and not source-model, geometry, material, or
pedestrian-position uncertainty. The route CDFs are conditional on the declared
fixed routes, which makes them fixed-route distributions rather than city
population distributions.
