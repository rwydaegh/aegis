# Dosimetry market case

## The correct investment test

The IOF commits EUR 250,000 over two years to answer a bounded technical and commercial question. It does not require a new venture to prove, before validation, that it will exceed the turnover of an established incumbent.

Three outcomes can independently justify the project:

- a durable specialist software company with approximately EUR 1 million in recurring revenue

- an embedded OEM licence or strategic acquisition

- a larger standalone workflow company if enterprise adoption and distribution succeed

The two funded StarTT applications reviewed set a materially lower commercial bar. The sensor application projected EUR 780,000 turnover in year five. The equine application specified a EUR 72.50 device plus an unpriced subscription, broad end-user assumptions, and partner-led distribution, but no bottom-up five-year revenue forecast. AEGIS should not be held to a requirement that was absent from these funded cases.

## Why ZMT turnover is the wrong ceiling

The quoted EUR 2 to 3 million ZMT figure comes from private-company data brokers and is not audited. Even if it is approximately correct, it describes ZMT's current business model. It does not cap the value of a different product sold at enterprise workflow level or distributed through several incumbent tools.

Selling low-price seats to individual dosimetrists would indeed be small. The larger product is a versioned design-to-conformance workflow:

- batch screening of antenna states, codebooks, body poses, and separations

- worst-case selection with an uncertainty guard band

- gradients and design optimisation

- standards-versioned reports and validation evidence

- on-premise SDK or API integration

- transfer of selected cases into FDTD/FEM and measurement systems

The buyer is therefore an antenna or device programme, test-lab workflow, or solver vendor, not one individual exposure researcher.

## Bottom-up scenarios

The figures below are hypotheses to validate, not contracted revenue. Embedded revenue covers customers or territories not billed directly, and data-only accounts exclude enterprise and laboratory customers.

| Revenue layer | Year 5 case | Year 7 to 9 breakout case |
|---|---:|---:|
| Device, chipset, and module programmes | 12 x EUR 120k = EUR 1.44M | 20 x EUR 175k = EUR 3.50M |
| APD-capable test laboratories | 18 x EUR 30k = EUR 0.54M | 30 x EUR 35k = EUR 1.05M |
| Embedded solver or compliance channels | 1 x EUR 400k = EUR 0.40M | 2 x EUR 500k = EUR 1.00M |
| Validation data and conformance updates | 20 x EUR 15k = EUR 0.30M | 25 x EUR 20k = EUR 0.50M |
| Regulators and specialist infrastructure cases | 4 x EUR 40k = EUR 0.16M | 6 x EUR 50k = EUR 0.30M |
| Total modeled recurring revenue | **EUR 2.84M** | **EUR 6.35M** |

The target universe is compatible with these account counts. GCF lists [more than 1,000 smartphone certifications over five years](https://www.globalcertificationforum.org/gcf-certified-products/smartphone-certifications.html) and hundreds of recent [module](https://www.globalcertificationforum.org/gcf-certified-products/module-certifications.html) and [router or fixed-wireless certifications](https://www.globalcertificationforum.org/gcf-certified-products/router-fwa-certifications.html). [CTIA reports more than 100 authorised laboratories](https://www.ctia.org/certification/eval_criteria/index.cfm), although only a subset has relevant APD capability. The local FCC analysis also shows that candidate filings above 6 GHz are concentrated among a small set of repeat applicants. This supports a 15 to 25 account enterprise target, not a mass-market sales plan.

## What must be proven

The EUR 2.84 million case becomes credible only after technical validation, paid pilots, and discovered enterprise pricing. The EUR 6.35 million case additionally requires two embedded channels and a support organisation capable of global enterprise delivery.

The IOF should test the following assumptions:

- AEGIS identifies the true worst cases with no unsafe false clearance after applying its uncertainty guard band.

- Screening removes enough full-wave iterations or design delay to justify programme-level rather than seat-level pricing.

- At least one device or chipset team owns a budget above EUR 100,000 for the complete workflow.

- At least one incumbent prefers licensing to internal reimplementation.

- A test laboratory can sell AEGIS-assisted pre-screening without weakening the independence of final certification.

These are appropriate project risks. They are not reasons to replace a mature dosimetry thesis with an unvalidated general EM proposition.
