The remainder of this paper is organized as follows.
\Cref{sec:law} derives the local absorption law at a visible surface
point. \Cref{sec:pB} reduces it to a near-constant scalar through
pseudo-Brewster compensation. Then \cref{sec:cauchy} integrates the
local law over the whole body. \Cref{sec:val} validates the theory
four ways. \Cref{sec:compliance} gives closed-form compliance bounds.
\Cref{sec:disc} discusses the regime of validity, and \cref{sec:conc}
concludes.
