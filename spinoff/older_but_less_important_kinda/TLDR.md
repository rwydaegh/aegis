## Career impact

**Short-term penalty:** Ex-founders get ~40% fewer callbacks when returning to corporate jobs (Kacperczyk & Younkin 2022). Lasts ~6 months.

**Long-term premium:** Ex-founders land positions ~3 years more senior than peers (Gompers et al., HBS). Even failed founders get this. Compounds over a career.

**The Brussels path:** Startup founder with PhD in telecom + industry experience = dream hire for ETNO, GSMA Europe, DG CONNECT, BEREC, or Brussels lobbying firms. The optimal sequence: PhD -> startup -> exit/acquisition -> 2 years at acquirer -> Brussels policy role by mid-30s.

## Jurisdiction

**Stay in Belgium.** The IID (3.75% IP tax) is better than Netherlands (9%), Ireland (6.25%), or Portugal (20%). VLAIO/istart/UGent support is worth more than any tax savings from moving.

**Delaware flip at seed round** (not now). When a US VC or accelerator requires it, create a Delaware C-Corp parent with the Belgian BV as subsidiary. Belgian subsidiary keeps VLAIO/IID eligibility. Standard playbook for ambitious European founders.

**EU Inc. (28th regime):** Proposal published March 2026. May be available by 2027-2028. Worth watching but not worth waiting for.
