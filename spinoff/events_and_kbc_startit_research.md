## KBC Start it -- the missing piece in your analysis

This is genuinely interesting and distinct from istart. Here's why:

**The model:** 12-month accelerator, **zero equity, zero cost, no strings attached**. This is unique in Belgium. They don't take shares, don't charge fees, don't make you a KBC customer.

**What you get:**
- Personalized coaching + mentor matching
- Co-working in Ghent (+ Antwerp, Brussels, Leuven, Hasselt, Kortrijk, and internationally)
- $1M+ in partner perks via GAN (Global Accelerator Network)
- Workshops, networking, international expansion support
- 3-min pitch + 12-min jury Q&A (no slides) for selection

**The new Start it Fund (Dec 2025):** KBC committed **EUR 100M**. Top ~5 startups per cohort (1% of applicants) get EUR 300-450K via convertible loan at end of program. Follow-on up to EUR 5M via KBC Securities.

**Track record:** 1,923 startups since 2014. EUR 1.1B raised collectively. 12,000+ jobs created. **73% survive after 5 years** (vs 51% international benchmark). Notable alumni: Aikido Security, Bolt, Loop Earplugs, Keyrock, Crazy Games.

**For you specifically:** Spring 2026 cohort started April 1 (applications closed). **Fall 2026 applications** will open later this year. Subscribe to the waitlist at [startit-x.com](https://startit-x.com/en/accelerate/start-it-kbc).

**How it compares to istart:**

| | Start it @KBC | imec.istart |
|---|---|---|
| Equity | None | EUR 100K convertible loan |
| Duration | 12 months | 12 months |
| Focus | All industries | Tech startups |
| University link | No | Yes (imec/knowledge institution) |
| Funding at entry | None (perks only) | EUR 100K |
| Later funding | EUR 300-450K (top 1%) | Up to EUR 300K follow-on |
| Co-working | 10 locations incl. Ghent | Leuven/Ghent |
| Selection | ~150 pitch, 1000 apply/year | ~30 selected/year |
| Best for | Networking, mentorship, perks | Cash + tech expertise + IP |

**Bottom line on KBC:** It's complementary to istart, not a replacement. You could do Start it @KBC for the free mentorship/network/perks AND apply to istart for the EUR 100K. They're not mutually exclusive. Start it is especially valuable for the co-founder search since you're embedded in a cohort of ~150 startups.
