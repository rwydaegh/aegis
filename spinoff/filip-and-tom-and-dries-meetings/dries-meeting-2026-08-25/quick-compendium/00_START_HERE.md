# AEGIS quick compendium

This folder is a browsing copy of the most useful AEGIS presentations, patent flowcharts, reusable figures and meeting context. Files are numbered so they remain in a useful order in a file browser.

## Fast route for the Dries meeting

1. Open `01_presentations/01_dries_current_deck.pdf` for the meeting deck.
2. Open `01_presentations/05_promotors_theory_master_deck.pdf` when a detailed derivation or appendix is needed.
3. Open `02_patent_flowcharts/00_flowchart_compendium.pdf` to flip through all ten selected flowchart pages.
4. Open `02_patent_flowcharts/01_alessandro_flowchart_options.pdf` for the three flowcharts prepared for Alessandro.
5. Open `03_reusable_figures/00_figure_compendium.pdf` to flip through all fifteen standalone figures.
6. Open `04_context/07_dries_speaker_notes.md` for anticipated objections and short answers.

## 03 Reusable figures

`00_figure_compendium.pdf` is a fifteen-page flip-through file. The remaining files are the same figures as individual vector PDFs for direct reuse. They cover Fresnel response, angular transmission and APD, skin depth, incidence geometry, validation, Mie asymptotics, the error budget and the coherent experiment. These are copied from existing presentations or the current Chapter 5 figure tree.
