Pose-information-gain ablation for §VII.E. Pose-aware (tier-A/B body orientation telemetry feeds the per-body $\bm{Q}$) vs.\ pose-ablate (T-pose Cauchy worst-case envelope on tier-A/B; tier-C is already Cauchy-on-detection in both), same seed, plaza-specular path model, binding regime at $43\,$dBm conducted transmit power and $E_{\mathrm{RL}} = 6\,$V/m. (a) ECDF of per-slot $\Delta$ sum-rate (aware $-$ ablate, Mbps), four precoders. MRT and worst-case back-off coincide with $\Delta = 0$ by construction (they do not consume $\bm{Q}$). The per-body oracle uses the posed $\bm{Q}$ unconditionally (it ignores the ablate flag) so its $\Delta\textsf{SR}$ is also identically zero, a useful sanity check. The proposed multi-body ECBF is bimodal: $35.7\%$ of slots at $\Delta\textsf{SR} < -1\,$Gbps (posed-$\bm{Q}$ in min-absorption fallback while the Cauchy variant remains feasible), $12.6\%$ at $\Delta\textsf{SR} > +1\,$Gbps (the opposite), $40.6\%$ within $|\Delta\textsf{SR}| < 100\,$Mbps. Median $\Delta\textsf{SR} = -78\,$Mbps, mean $-3.1\,$Gbps. (b) Per-body $P_{\mathrm{abs}}$ ECDF for the multi-body ECBF, aware vs.\ ablate. The pose-aware curve dominates: $0.96\%$ aware violations vs $4.58\%$ ablate ($4.7\times$ compliance gain). The Cauchy envelope's looser feasible set lets the maximizer push absorbed power up against the budget when the T-pose envelope under-estimates the true posed absorption; the pose-aware solver, working against the tighter posed operator, holds margin in hand at the cost of more frequent solver fallback ($83.6\%$ aware vs $55.1\%$ ablate, both with warm-started $\bm{\lambda}^{(t)}$ from $\bm{\lambda}^{(t-1)}$ and the $\textsf{max\_outer}=8$ cap of \cref{sec:multibody-ecbf}). Pose telemetry buys exposure margin and pays it back in solver fallback under the present cap; the proposed solver should approach the oracle's $26.3\%$ fallback rate once the Cauchy-on-tier-C envelope is replaced with a tighter Lanczos-projected operator.
