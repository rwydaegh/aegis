- Ericsson (2021 Oct). White Paper GFTL-21:000987, "Accurately assessing

  Available at `ericsson.com/495136/assets/local/reports-papers/white-papers/5g-and-emf.pdf`.
- Ericsson research paper. "RF EMF Exposure from Array Antennas in 5G
  Equipment." At `ericsson.com/en/reports-and-papers/research-papers/rf-emf-exposure-from-array-antennas-in-5g-equipment`.
- Ericsson research paper. "Implications of EMF exposure limits on output
  power levels for 5G devices above 6 GHz." At
  `ericsson.com/en/reports-and-papers/research-papers/implications-of-emf-exposure-limits-on-output-power-levels-for-5g-devices-above-6-ghz`.
- Ericsson research paper. "EMF Exposure evaluation of Distributed MIMO."
  At `ericsson.com/en/reports-and-papers/research-papers/emf-evaluation-industrial-indoor-environment`.
- Colombi, Di Paola, Joshi, Xu, Bischoff, et al. (2025). "Network-based
  Assessment of Actual EIRP of 5G BS in a Stadium with 100,000 People and
  Implications on EMF Compliance." IEEE Antennas and Wireless Propagation
