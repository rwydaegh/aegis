# Manuscript verb audit

## Scope and method

This audit covers the abstract, main text, captions, availability statement,
and acknowledgment in `build/main.tex`. It treats auxiliaries and modal verbs
as verbs. It groups inflected forms under one lemma. It excludes bibliography
titles, equations, table cells, nouns such as *building* and *ray tracing*, and
participial adjectives such as *selected*, *shadowed*, *controlled*, and
*paired*. The source was checked automatically for candidates and then read
sentence by sentence to remove part-of-speech errors and restore missed verbs.

The audit led to direct edits before this table was frozen. In particular,
*supplies agentic labeling* became the more precise *assigns material labels*,
*specular path is largest* became *specular component is largest*, and scope
sentences now use author agency where it is clearer. No unusual verb remains
only for style.

## Final judgment

The manuscript uses mostly concrete physical or computational verbs. The few
abstract subjects with agency are conventional and literal, such as a figure
that **shows**, a table that **lists**, or a model that **includes** a stated
interaction. The audit found no remaining metaphorical action such as results
that *speak*, frameworks that *unlock*, or methods that *navigate* a problem.
