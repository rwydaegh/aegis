Pareto-active hero for §VII.D. Same plaza scenario as Fig.~\ref{fig:hero_pareto}, but at tx 43\,dBm and a $0.1\times$ per-body budget (16.34\,mW), with bodies entering and leaving via Brussels Grand Place street nodes. (a) ECDFs of $P_{\mathrm{abs}}/L_{\mathrm{RL}}$ across body-slots separate cleanly per precoder: MRT pushes past the budget on 65.8\,\% of slots, WC back-off straddles it (median $0.36$), ZF sits at $5\times10^{-3}$ via interference-nulling DOFs ($M=64 \gg K=25$), and multi-body ECBF returns the min-absorption fallback (zero rate, zero absorbed power) on 100\,\% of slots when the solver's $10^{-3}$ residual tolerance flags the QCQP infeasible. (b) Per-precoder violation rate vs.\ mean sum-rate. ZF dominates the frontier at $(0.1\,\%, 69.2\,\text{Gbps})$; MRT and WC back-off saturate the SE cap at $7.2$\,Gbps but split on compliance ($65.8\,\%$ vs $2.0\,\%$); ECBF and oracle yield $14$ and $150$\,Mbps respectively. The binding-regime takeaway is that compliance is achievable for free via ZF in M\,$\gg$\,K configurations; the §IV QCBP solver's value emerges in regimes the §VII.A scenario does not exercise.
