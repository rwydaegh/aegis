## The disclosure landmine

This is the single highest-stakes item in the entire analysis and the current email draft walks straight into it.

### EPO absolute novelty, applied to your ask

From `patent_decision_and_defense_mechanics.md` line 29:

> EPO enforces absolute novelty (no grace period for inventor's own disclosures, unlike US). Every filing anywhere in the world after this date is later than Robin's. **This is the only step that has to happen before the PhD defense.** EPO absolute novelty means a public defense = disclosure = permanent priority loss if priority is filed after it.

The same rule applies to any public presentation at BioEM — poster, demo, exhibition booth, workshop session, oral slot. All of those are public disclosures. If you present novel substance before priority is filed, you permanently lose patent rights worldwide. Unrecoverable.

### Realistic timeline math

- Today: 17 April 2026.
- BioEM 2026: roughly early-to-mid June 2026 (verify exact dates).
- TechTransfer-to-filing realistic window: **2-4 months** per `patent_decision_and_defense_mechanics.md` §Stage 1.
- Gap to BioEM: ~6-8 weeks. On the optimistic end of TechTransfer's timeline. Feasible but tight, and you do not yet have a filing date confirmed in writing.

Until you have that confirmation in writing from TechTransfer, **assume no safe public presentation at BioEM**.

### What's still safe

- **Attending as registrant**, listening, taking notes, collecting business cards.
- **1:1 private conversations**, even substantive ones, with individuals under implicit professional confidentiality. Not public disclosure under EPO.
- **Asking questions during Q&A** about others' work.
- **Presenting previously-published material** (e.g. IEEE Access 2022 or npj Wireless Technology 2026 content that is already public).

### What's not safe before priority is filed

- Posters, oral slots, demo booths, workshop contributions, ad hoc evening presentations, handing out printed figures, sharing pre-submission manuscripts with people who aren't under NDA, talking freely to journalists, or tweeting about it from the venue.

## The Azadeh email, dissected

### The ask is a disclosure vector

Your current ask is: "poster, demo, exhibition, workshop, some exception, whatever works." Every single item in that list is a public disclosure under EPO rules. Before priority is filed, this is the wrong ask. After priority is filed, you can narrow to one specific format.

### The overclaim

> "it replaces FDTD entirely for numerical dosimetry above 6 GHz"

`honest_analysis.md` Q8 explicitly says:

> Rebuild the primary pitch around device mmWave APD pre-compliance, not base-station compliance. [...] The pitch is not "replace Sim4Life" but "accelerate pre-screening by 100-1000x, then feed the worst-case scenarios into Sim4Life for rigorous validation."

Azadeh's professional network overlaps with Kuster, ZMT, and the FDTD establishment. Claiming to replace FDTD will land as either naive or adversarial. Neither opens doors.

Also: the validation claim ("within a few percent of published numerical results") is true for the cases you've tested, but a matched AEGIS-vs-Sim4Life run on a standard IEC 63195-2 benchmark phone geometry at 28 GHz does not yet exist. `honest_analysis.md` Q3 flags this as "essential before pitching Apple or ZMT." Pitching it to the BioEM chair is in the same reference class. Soften the language.

### The patent mystique

> "I cannot explain all the details because we are in the process of patenting what is patentable."

In pharma / medtech this phrasing is normal. In the academic EM community, which is culturally closer to physics than to biotech, it reads as "I'm prioritizing IP over science." That's a red flag for a senior researcher who has built a career on open method exchange. Replace with the factual variant: "full details are in a manuscript under review; a patent application is being prepared in parallel with UGent TechTransfer." Same information, no mystique.

### The weak opener

> "I missed the Cairns abstract deadline but wanted to ask if there's still a way to be there"

Apologetic, supplicant, puts you on the back foot. Azadeh reads a lot of these. Open with credibility instead.

### The girlfriend framing

> "My girlfriend Carolina Camacho Cadena from our group will already be at BioEM on the ARPANSA grant."

Two problems. First, "girlfriend" is a personal register in a professional email. Second, the relationship isn't the signal — the signal is that a colleague from Wout's group is already presenting at BioEM on an ARPANSA grant, which demonstrates the group's existing engagement with the conference and with Australian regulators. Frame it that way.

### Missing credentials

Not in the draft but should be:
- Wout Joseph's name up front. Azadeh probably knows him personally and through ICES/ICNIRP circles.
- Two prior BioEM prizes. You're not a stranger; you're a recognized contributor.
- First-author publications (IEEE Access 2022, npj Wireless Technology 2026). Establishes technical track record without claiming a breakthrough.
- Sim4Life competition placement. Shows you respect the ecosystem you're trying to engage with.

### Other cleanup

- Typo: "worshop" → "workshop".
- Verify Azadeh's actual BioEM role. BEMS / EBEA leadership rotates. She may be program chair, local chair, board member, or president — get it right.
- "On my end, I can adapt my message to any of those formats" reads as needy. Drop.
- "Whatever works" is similarly weak. Ask for one specific thing.
