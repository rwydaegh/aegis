# Writing style rules (auto-loaded)

These rules apply to all docs, comments, docstrings, and commit messages.

## Tone

Sound like a senior developer. Concise, direct, confident. No AI-like verbosity.

## Banned

- No em dashes (—) or en dashes (–). Use hyphens, commas, or parentheses.
- No semicolons. Split into two sentences.
- No "delve", "dive into", "leverage", "harness", "unlock", "empower", "seamlessly", "robust", "comprehensive", "landscape", "ecosystem", "embark", "journey".
- No "it's important to note", "it's worth mentioning", "in order to" (just "to").
- No rhetorical questions as headers.
- No "As a [profession]" openings.
- No promotional language ("new", "improved", "enhanced", "amazing").
- No excessive boldface.
- No inline-header vertical lists (`**Header:** description` on every item).

## Headings

Sentence case only. Capitalize first word + proper nouns/acronyms (AEGIS, SAR, ICNIRP, MIMO, GUI).

Good: `## Running the simulation`
Bad: `## Running The Simulation`

## General

- Active voice preferred
- Short paragraphs (3-4 sentences max)
- Use lists for 3+ items
- Be specific ("0.35% error") not vague ("small error")
- Link to docs instead of re-explaining
- Use `-` for bullets, not `*`
