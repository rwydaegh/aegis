Binding-regime hero for §VII.D. Plaza scenario at $43\,$dBm conducted transmit power on the $8\times 8$ panel, $E_{\mathrm{RL}} = 6\,$V/m post-2014 Brussels arr\^et\'e / Italy outdoor attention level (per-body $L_{\mathrm{RL}} = 27.7\,$mW), 9000-slot 5-min realistic-walk trace. (a) ECDFs of $P_{\mathrm{abs}}/L_{\mathrm{RL}}$ across body-slots separate cleanly per precoder: MRT exceeds the budget on $14.9\%$ of slots (median $0.51 L_{\mathrm{RL}}$, $99^{\mathrm{th}}$ percentile $1.77 L_{\mathrm{RL}}$), WC back-off scales MRT down to $0.32 L_{\mathrm{RL}}$ median ($2.0\%$ violations, residual from rare per-body hot ridges the worst-body backoff does not see), ZF sits at $0.04 L_{\mathrm{RL}}$ median via interference-nulling DOFs ($M=64 \gg K=25$, $0.10\%$ body-slot violations but with rare $3.4\times$ overshoots where a body crosses a geometric null), the proposed multi-body ECBF returns the min-absorption fallback on $83.6\%$ of slots (where the Cauchy-on-tier-C envelope is too tight for the $\textsf{max\_outer}=8$ Newton sweep), and the per-body oracle holds $0.28 L_{\mathrm{RL}}$ median with $26.3\%$ fallback. (b) Per-precoder violation rate vs.\ mean sum-rate. ZF dominates the frontier at $(0.10\%, 74.0\,\text{Gbps})$; MRT and WC back-off split compliance at the same $11.2\,$Gbps SE cap; oracle and ECBF yield $11.8$ and $2.5\,$Gbps respectively, the ECBF result driven by the high min-absorption fallback rate. The binding-regime takeaway is that compliance is achievable for free via ZF in $M\gg K$ configurations once the budget is loose enough that the geometric nulls keep the body-slot tail bounded; the §IV QCQP solver's value emerges on the per-body absorption side rather than on aggregate sum-rate, where the warm-started Newton sweep needs uncapping or a tighter Cauchy-on-tier-C replacement to close the residual fallback gap.
