% PREV: Street-level radiofrequency exposure varies along a pedestrian route because
% PREV: buildings change the direct and reflected fields, surface materials affect how
% PREV: much power returns to the street, and the human body absorbs differently
% PREV: depending on the direction of arrival. This study projects 360-degree street
% PREV: images onto a photogrammetric city mesh to identify surface materials along
% PREV: pedestrian routes at 15~GHz. Transmitters are placed along the visible
% PREV: roofline in proportion to its physical length. All results are normalized per
% PREV: unit product of areal source density and effective isotropic radiated power
% PREV: (EIRP). The transport model treats direct
% PREV: paths and one specular reflection exactly, estimates one diffuse reflection, and
% PREV: stops. The arriving fields and their directions are then applied to a
% PREV: 56,024-element Duke body model. A geometric fixed-grid diagnostic covers ten
% PREV: urban locations in Europe, Latin America, and East Asia and finds a twofold
% PREV: span in location-median whole-body SAR. Five of these locations are then studied
% PREV: with image-derived materials along fixed pedestrian routes containing 73
% PREV: observation points, each calculated with 16 independent replicas of 200,000
% PREV: primary rays and 4,096 angular output cells. In a controlled test scene, the
% PREV: adjoint estimate and deterministic quadrature differ by at most 0.0616~dB for
% PREV: the reflected term. An independent forward tracer gives a maximum difference
% PREV: of 0.0344~dB. Route-median whole-body specific absorption rate differs by a
% PREV: factor of 13.34 across the five routes. Direct paths carry the largest share at
% PREV: all 67 points with line of sight, while the diffuse reflection is the only
% PREV: nonzero modeled contribution at six fully shadowed points. The maximum change
% PREV: from 12 to 16 replicas is 0.0436~dB, though lower-tail estimates in Mexico
% PREV: City and Tokyo are less stable. These results describe ten fixed-grid locations
% PREV: and five selected routes under a fixed model and do not estimate city-wide or
% PREV: deployed-network exposure.
% PREV: \end{abstract}
\begin{keywords}
Body dosimetry, image-informed propagation, ray tracing, street imagery, urban
propagation, whole-body specific absorption rate.
