## Key Gaps Identified

Based on comprehensive analysis, the following topics are **NOT covered** or **underexplored**:

### High-Confidence Gaps (No close matches)

1. **Admitting ignorance / saying "I don't know"** - No columns on intellectual humility as practice
2. **Performing busyness** - Burnout columns exist but not about the performance aspect
3. **Seminar Q&A dynamics** - No columns on performative question-asking
4. **Randomness of success** - One column on "engineering luck" but not on confronting contingency

## Corpus Characteristics

### Tone
- First-person, conversational
- Honest but generally optimistic
- Practical with personal anchors
- Avoids toxic positivity but offers hope

### Structure (typical)
1. Personal story/hook
2. Problem identification
3. Journey or insight
4. Practical takeaways
5. Broader implications

### Audience
- PhD students
- Postdocs
- Early-career researchers
- PIs (for management columns)

### What Gets Published
- Specific personal experiences with transferable lessons
- Counter-narratives that challenge assumptions
- Practical guides with personality
- Honest struggles with resolution or acceptance
- Underrepresented perspectives made visible

### What Likely Doesn't Get Published
- Pure complaints without insight
- Generic advice without personal anchor
- Topics already extensively covered
- Too niche (only relevant to one subfield)
- Controversial without nuance
