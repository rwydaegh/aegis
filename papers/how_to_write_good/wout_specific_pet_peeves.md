Black non rounded rectangles with text (same fontsize) in the middle. arrows shoot out of these boxes to other boxes. You can, if you want, collect the boxes in dashed lines to label a collection of those boxes to be part of something high level. This is the kind of language for flowcharts that Wout adores. Basic, crystal clear, no fluff. BUT clean. Sooo, that means generate --> png --> read --> critique extremely hard --> iterate, until satisfied. Have a good think first if it's useful here and what it would be about. How it fit in the text (needs explanation of course).

3. Extremely precise on the use of ~. Adds it almost everywhere. Not just the obvious places, but nearly every number ever has one of these ~, or before a unit, and much much more. 
4. Wout is lawful neutral, he wants the paper to be normal and will circle any kind of weird sentence that isn't absolute deadpan straightforward science. Even words that are considered weird.
5. The abstract is quite prescriptive. It needs to be very dense. Needs to show key results. The quantities cited (numbers) require a remarkable amount of precision rather than 'just throwing out a number'. An error is not just 'an error', it's quite precise even in the abstract.
