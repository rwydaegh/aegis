# Figure Regeneration Notes

This PaperMaker instance is intended to regenerate figures from files inside
`TAP_paper` only. Do not import helpers or data from sibling repositories.

## Local instructions that matter

- `README.md` treats `figures/` as the manuscript artifact directory and
  `scripts/` as the figure-generation snapshot.
- `THIS_PAPER.md` says to keep edits traceable and avoid direct paper prose
  edits outside the active task.
- `papers/how_to_write_good/figures_extreme_quality.md` requires a rendered
  PNG check after figure edits; PDF source alone is not verification.
