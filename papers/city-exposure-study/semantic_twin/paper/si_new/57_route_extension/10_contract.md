The route-based extension applies the production contract to five additional
prepared scenes and combines them with the original five routes in one
authenticated report. The ten selected routes contain 163 observation points.
Every route uses image-derived surface properties, a Duke phantom facing along
the walk, 64 independent seeds from 7 through 70, 200,000 primary rays per
point and seed, and 4,096 first-diffuse output cells. Direct, exact order-1
specular, and first-diffuse transport use the same first-material model as the
five detailed routes. The extension changes the site and route coverage. It
does not turn the selected routes into a population sample or a city ranking.
