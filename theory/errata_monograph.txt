Erratum 1: Bamba 2014 bibliography entry points to the wrong paper
------------------------------------------------------------------

Location: bibliography, \bibitem{Bamba2014}, lines 5798--5804.

Current (incorrect) entry:

    \bibitem{Bamba2014}
      A.~Bamba, W.~Joseph, G.~Vermeeren, E.~Tanghe, D.~P.~Gaillot,
      J.~B.~Andersen, J.~{\O}.~Nielsen, M.~Li\'{e}nard, and L.~Martens,
      ``Validation of experimental whole-body SAR assessment method in a complex
      indoor environment,'' \textit{Bioelectromagnetics}, vol.~34, no.~2,
      pp.~122--132, 2013.
      \doi{10.1002/bem.21749}

This is the BEM 2013 paper, which is a chamber-method validation paper at
2.8 GHz only (verified by reading papers/key_literature/other/4229389.pdf).
It does not contain the eta(f) regression, the 1.45-5.8 GHz fit, or the
ellipsoidal-phantom FDTD that the monograph attaches to this key (e.g.
lines 627-631, 795-796, 847-853, 3750-3753).

Correct entry (the paper that actually contains those results):

    \bibitem{Bamba2014}
      A.~Bamba, W.~Joseph, G.~Vermeeren, A.~Thielens, E.~Tanghe, and
      L.~Martens, ``A formula for human average whole-body SAR$_{\mathrm{wb}}$
      under diffuse fields exposure in the GHz region,''
      \textit{Phys.\ Med.\ Biol.}, vol.~59, no.~23, pp.~7435--7456, 2014.
      \doi{10.1088/0031-9155/59/23/7435}

The same bug existed in papers/TAP_paper/paper.tex and has been fixed there
(commit on the master branch; before-state reproduces this bib entry verbatim).

Action required: replace the \bibitem{Bamba2014} block in monograph_v2.tex
with the corrected entry above. No body-text claims need to change; the
quantitative content already matches the PMB 2014 paper.


Erratum 2: Approximation 2 error is understated ~6x (alpha-beta swap)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Discovered 2026-08-08 during round-2 review of the TWC coherent-exposure
paper (see papers/coherent-exposure-operator, WOUT_RESPONSE.md round 2 and
versions_and_reviews/v1/approximations_deep_dive.md for the full analysis).

Location: prop:approx2 and its figure (0.44 % / 0.17 % at 28 GHz), the
App. 2 column of tab:approx-accuracy, the trend sentence below it, and
the summary paragraph near line 1175 ("exact to 0.44 %").

Cause: in theory/scripts/validate_approx2.py the helper alpha_beta()
returns (beta, alpha) -- its own comment says so -- but Gamma() unpacks
the tuple as (alpha, beta). The roles of attenuation and phase constant
are therefore swapped. The swapped computation reproduces the printed
0.44 % / 0.17 % exactly; the monograph's algebra (eq:Lambda, eq:Gamma)
is correct and unaffected.

Correct values, |1 - Gamma| max/mean over (theta, theta') in [0, 85] deg^2,
skin dielectrics from tab:skin-data:

    f (GHz)   correct max/mean (%)   printed
       6          4.14 / 1.65          --
      10          3.28 / 1.31        <= 0.30
      20          2.55 / 1.02          --
      28          2.54 / 1.01        <= 0.44
      40          2.81 / 1.12        <= 0.55
      60          3.56 / 1.42        <= 0.75
     100          5.53 / 2.21        <= 1.1

The frequency trend is also wrong: the correct error is U-shaped with a
minimum near 20-28 GHz, not monotone increasing. Toward low frequency
the loss tangent drops, alpha shrinks relative to beta, and the 1/(2 alpha)
normalisation amplifies the phase spread; toward high frequency |n|
drops and the refraction-angle spread grows.

The error is a first-order pure-phase term, (beta_n' - beta_n)/(2 alpha).
It cancels to second order by referencing each path's phase at the mean
absorption depth zbar = 1/(2 alpha) instead of the surface (per-path
weight sqrt(Lambda_nn) exp(-i beta_n zbar), one common zbar). Residual
error is below 0.05 % at 28 GHz and below 0.2 % up to 100 GHz, at no
structural cost. Full derivation and an approximation ladder (including
a universal rank-2 scheme exact to 1e-5) in
papers/coherent-exposure-operator/versions_and_reviews/v1/approximations_deep_dive.md.

Action required: fix the unpack in validate_approx2.py (done in the same
commit as this erratum), regenerate the figure, update prop:approx2, the
figure caption, tab:approx-accuracy, and the trend sentence. Consider
adopting the mean-depth phase referencing as the definition, as the TWC
paper now does.


Erratum 3: Approximation 1 cross-term bound is unsound
------------------------------------------------------

Discovered 2026-05-11 by the multi-agent physics audit of jsac2_v3.tex
(JSAC2/paper/agent_reports/: audit_theory_2026-05-11.md Issue 3,
AUDIT_SYNTHESIS_2026-05-11.md T3, agent_C_physics.md, audit_approx1b.py).
Recorded here 2026-08-09; the monograph was never updated after the audit.

Location: prop:approx1 ("O(1/|n|^2) <= 4 % on TM-TM cross-terms"), the
App. 1 column of tab:approx-accuracy, and the summary paragraph near
line 1175.

Cause: the proof argues sin(theta_t) sin(theta_t') = O(1/|n|^2) and
asserts the substitution error inherits that scaling. But the
substitution error is sin(theta) sin(theta') (1 - 1/|n|^2), which is
O(1) because the incident product is O(1). Measured: inner-product
mismatch up to 90 % at (0, 85) deg, depth-integrated TM-TM cross-term
error up to 35 % at (0, 75) deg.

The rescue lives on the regulated observable, not the per-pair bound:
theory/scripts/validate_approx1.py (committed 2026-06-18, 70b4ee46)
measures the error on the surface-integrated operator Q, where the
surface phase integral decorrelates the widely separated pairs. Result:
point median 48.7 % / max 110.5 %, 4 cm^2 patch median 5.7 % / max
15.0 %, whole body below 0.1 %.

Action required: either restate prop:approx1 on the integrated operator
with the measured numbers, or drop the approximation and use the exact
refracted direction e'_p (free, since the channel entries are complex
either way). The TWC paper does the latter.
