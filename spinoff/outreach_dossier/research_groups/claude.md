# Who actually does the world's RF-EMF numerical dosimetry

The global RF-EMF computational dosimetry community is remarkably small — perhaps **30 individuals occupy overlapping leadership roles** across ICNIRP, IEEE ICES TC95/SC6, and IEC TC106 — and it is organized along a clear Switzerland–Japan–Belgium–France–Italy–Australia axis. The Americas have largely ceded leadership over the past decade: **US academic FDTD pioneers (Taflove, Gandhi) are emeritus, Brooks AFB's bioeffects program has been downsized, and IEEE ICES SC6 is now co-chaired by Japan and China rather than the US**. Meanwhile a parallel, communications-engineering-driven wave of work — ray-tracing/digital-twin/ML for exposure maps — is emerging mostly from wireless-networks groups (NVIDIA Sionna RT, INSA Lyon, Northeastern, Tor Vergata, Surrey), with **Wiart's Chaire C2M at Télécom Paris and CNR-IEIIT Milan being the principal bridges** between this new community and traditional dosimetry. What follows is a ranked, honest map of the field, with focus tags: **[NUM]** numerical/computational, **[MEAS]** measurement, **[ML/DT]** machine-learning/digital-twin, **[EPI]** epidemiology (adjacent).

## Tier 1: the five groups that effectively define the field

**1. IT'IS Foundation, Zurich — Niels Kuster** **[NUM, MEAS, ML/DT]**. The single most important group globally. IT'IS, with its commercial siblings ZMT Zurich MedTech (Sim4Life) and SPEAG (DASY measurement platforms), employs roughly **70–90 staff combined, with ~30–40 active dosimetry researchers**. They built Sim4Life and its predecessor SEMCAD-X, which is the de facto computational platform used by virtually every other group in this report. They built the **Virtual Population (Duke, Ella, Billie, Thelonious, Roberta, Yoon-sun, Jeduk, plus 25+ models, now V4.1 with 2,200+ neuro-functionalized nerve trajectories)** — the anatomical models the entire field runs simulations on. They built the DASY8 APD measurement module for 24–30 GHz. Senior scientists include Esra Neufeld (computational physics), Manuel Murbach (mobile/wearable), Andreas Christ (mmWave/APD), Myles Capstick (experimental), and Marie-Christine Gosselin (ViP). Kuster holds central seats in IEC TC106, IEEE ICES SC34, and the IEEE C95.1 revision that introduced APD above 6 GHz. They host IEC TC106/IEEE ICES TC34 meetings (Vienna, March 2026).

**2. Akimasa Hirata Lab, Nagoya Institute of Technology, Japan** **[NUM, ML/DT]**. The world's most influential single-PI dosimetry lab. Hirata became **Chair of ICNIRP in July 2024 (first Asian chair)** and is **Chair of IEEE ICES TC95 SC6 (Computational Dosimetry)**. Group size is roughly 15–25 (PhDs, postdocs, RAs). Key affiliated faculty: Sachiko Kodera (thermal + ML), Jose Gomez-Tames (now Chiba), Essam Rashed (now Univ. of Hyogo, deep-learning head models — **ForkNet, SHARM, CondNet**), Yinliang Diao (joint with South China Agricultural Univ., now IEEE SC6 co-chair), Kun Li (now UEC Tokyo, chair of SC6 TG6 on EPD/APD averaging). Methodologies span in-house FDTD scaled to HPC, scalar-potential FDTD, Pennes bio-heat coupling, Bayesian uncertainty, and deep-learning surrogates. **Hirata's 2021 *Phys. Med. Biol.* review on dosimetry above 6 GHz is the foundational citation for ICNIRP 2020 and IEEE C95.1-2019**, and his 2026 *IEEE J. Microwaves* review on model variability and the 2025 *Bioelectromagnetics* SC6 workshop synthesis (Diao, Joseph, Poljak, Giaccone, Kodera, Laakso et al.) effectively functions as the membership list of the active international community.

**3. NICT EMC Laboratory, Tokyo — Soichi Watanabe, Kanako Wake, Kensuke Sasaki** **[NUM, MEAS]**. Roughly 15–20 researchers. NICT created the **TARO (adult male) and HANAKO (adult female) Japanese voxel models** — among the most widely used non-Western anatomical models worldwide. They publish the Sasaki Cole–Cole dielectric tissue database used by everyone doing mmWave dosimetry. Teruo Onishi (NICT) became **Chair of IEC TC106 in 2024**, succeeding Mike Wood (Telstra/AMTA). The group is the global anchor for measurement-based validation and ran the 28 GHz 5G base-station monitoring campaign in Tokyo (2024). Watanabe co-authored ICNIRP 2020.

**4. WAVES, Ghent University / imec, Belgium — Wout Joseph** **[MEAS, NUM, ML/DT]**. The user's own group; ranked honestly at fourth globally and arguably **first in the world specifically for 5G base-station in-situ exposure assessment with beamforming/massive MIMO**. The broader WAVES group is ~50–70 researchers; the EMF-exposure sub-team is ~10–15. Joseph anchors a team including Luc Martens, Emmeric Tanghe, Günter Vermeeren, Sam Aerts (now EPRI), Arno Thielens, Reza Aminzadeh, Kenneth Deprez, Leen Verloock, Bram Stroobandt, Margot Deruyck (also COST INTERACT grant holder), Serge Shikhantsov (hybrid ray-tracer + FDTD), Thomas Tarnaud (neuron models, chairs SC6 TG8), Jeroen Van der Straeten. **The spatial-duty-cycle and code-selective measurement procedures for 5G NR that Ghent developed are now embedded in IEC 62232.** Joseph sits on the IEEE ICES SC6 Advisory Board (created Jan 2025) alongside Laakso, Poljak, and Yamazaki. Recent flagship outputs: Deprez et al. *Bioelectromagnetics* 46:e70019 (2025) on 5G spectral exposure across four European countries; Aerts et al. *Sensors* (2023) on 5G NR small cells; the Van der Straeten low-cost triaxial 5G sensor (2025). Heavy collaborator with Swiss TPH on epidemiology dose calculations and with Ericsson Research on commercial-network measurement.

**5. Chair C2M, Télécom Paris (Institut Polytechnique de Paris) — Joe Wiart** **[NUM, ML/DT]**. Small in headcount (~5–8 faculty + 5–10 PhDs/postdocs) but enormously influential. Wiart, ex-head of Orange Labs dosimetry (1997–2015), **chairs CENELEC TC106X** (the European mirror of IEC TC106) and is the conceptual founder of **stochastic dosimetry** — polynomial chaos expansion, sparse polynomial chaos, low-rank tensor approximations, and polynomial-chaos-Kriging hybrids — documented in his 2016 Wiley monograph. With Shanshan Wang (assistant professor, AI/ML in dosimetry) and collaborators at ETH Zurich (Bruno Sudret, UQLab) and CNR-IEIIT Milan, C2M is the most important group worldwide for the **AI/ML side of dosimetry**: neural-network SAR surrogates, ExposNet-style deep-learning EMF mapping, and stochastic-geometry + EMF analyses for 5G EN-DC.

## Tier 2: major specialty groups and national champions

**6. CNR-IEIIT Milan + Politecnico di Milano — Marta Parazzini** **[NUM, ML/DT]**. ~8–12 researchers including Serena Fiocchi, Emma Chiaramello, Marta Bonato, Gabriella Tognola, Silvia Gallucci, Martina Benini, with senior advisor Paolo Ravazzani. Italy's flagship for **stochastic numerical dosimetry of 5G beamforming, RIS, V2X, and mmWave wearables**; key collaborator of Wiart and Ghent. Notable 2024–2025 papers on RIS exposure (*IEEE Open J. Antennas Propag.*), wearable mmWave patch arrays with IETR (*IEEE Access*), and anthropomorphic skin stratification for APD (*Sensors* 25:4461, 2025).

**7. IETR/eWaves, University of Rennes / CNRS — Maxim Zhadobov** **[NUM, MEAS]**. The European reference for **millimeter-wave (FR2/FR3) computational and experimental dosimetry**. Zhadobov is president of URSI France Commission K and active in IEC TC106 and IEEE TC95.4. Group of ~10–15 with Ronan Sauleau, Philippe Besnier, Giulia Sacco, Ante Lojić Kapetanović, Mohamed Ziane. Built one of the first mmWave reverberation chambers and bio-inspired mmWave phantoms. Hosted BioEM 2025.

**8. ARPANSA + Swinburne + Wollongong (ACEBR), Australia — Ken Karipidis, Sarah Loughran, Andrew Wood, Rodney Croft** **[MEAS, EPI, NUM]**. The dominant Anglophone voice in ICNIRP guidance: **Karipidis is ICNIRP Vice-Chair (2024–2028)**, Loughran is on the Commission, Wood chaired the ICNIRP dosimetry project group, and Croft was ICNIRP Chair until Hirata succeeded him in 2024. ACEBR (NHMRC-funded, ~25+ researchers across institutions) operates the Swinburne RF Dosimetry Lab and led the influential **2021 *JESEE* "state-of-the-science" review of 5G mobile networks and health above 6 GHz**. Strong in measurement, animal exposure systems, and provocation studies; less strong in pure ML/digital-twin numerical work.

**9. Aalto University, Finland — Ilkka Laakso** **[NUM]**. Small group, strategically central. **Laakso joined the ICNIRP Main Commission in July 2024**, sits on the IEEE ICES SC6 Advisory Board, and was a co-author of the Hirata 2021 review and the **2026 *Phys. Med. Biol.* "Roadmap towards personalized approaches and safety considerations in non-ionizing radiation"** (Laakso et al.) — the field's recent consensus document. Specializes in rigorous LF and RF computational dosimetry with anisotropic muscle modeling and intercomparison studies.

**10. University of Split, Croatia — Dragan Poljak** **[NUM]**. Mid-sized group (with Mario Cvetković, Hrvoje Dodig, Maja Škiljo) but high impact through **integral-equation/BEM methods** for mmWave APD/TPD/IPD analytical and numerical analysis. Poljak holds the IEEE EMC Society Technical Achievement Award (2019) and the George Green Medal (2021); he sits on the IEEE ICES SC6 Advisory Board. The task brief's original list missed this group — it belongs in the top tier of European computational dosimetry.

**11. La Sapienza, Rome — Micaela Liberti, Francesca Apollonio, Marta Cavagnaro** **[NUM]**. Long-established Italian bioelectromagnetics group (Cavagnaro is an Italian FDTD pioneer since the 1990s). Strong on FDTD, hyperthermia/ablation, mmWave UE exposure, and dielectric tissue characterization. Members are on the ICNIRP Scientific Expert Group and the RF Project Group.

**12. INRIM, Turin — Oriano Bottauscio, Luca Zilberti** **[NUM, MEAS]**. Italian National Metrology Institute. Distinctive for **parallel BEM and FE-BE solvers** for voxel models, MRI/WPT safety dosimetry, and metrology-grade uncertainty frameworks. Less focused on 5G base stations than on providing the reference uncertainty methodology others validate against. Sits within CENELEC TC106X WG3.

**13. ETRI, Daejeon, Korea — Hyung-Do Choi, Ae-Kyoung Lee** **[NUM, MEAS]**. ~10–15 in Radio Environment & Monitoring division. Korea's national focal point: developed Korean adult and 7-year-old child voxel models, runs 28 GHz and 3.5 GHz in-vitro exposure systems, supports the Korean RRA SAR labelling regime, and was an active MOBI-KIDS dosimetry partner. Lee's 2023 *Environ. Res.* paper on downlink dose in Korean networks is a current benchmark.

**14. University of Rome Tor Vergata — Luca Chiaraviglio, Stefania Bartoletti** **[MEAS, ML/DT]**. ~5–10 in the EMF/network sub-team. Bridges communications engineering and EMF: produces the most-cited recent work on **5G smartphone vs. base-station exposure dominance** (*IEEE TMC* 23(3) 2024) and EMF-aware network design (with Elzanaty/Surrey and Alouini/KAUST). Hosts the Fondazione RESTART 5G testbed and led a 34-lab Italian national 5G EMF intercomparison.

**15. Seibersdorf Laboratories, Austria — Gernot Schmid** **[NUM, MEAS]**. Accredited applied lab, ~10–20 staff. Bridges Sim4Life-based numerical dosimetry with accredited SAR/EMF/mmWave measurement; the German-speaking regulatory community's go-to subcontractor for BfS-commissioned studies. Active in IEC TC106 and IEEE ICES.

## Tier 3: specialized, emerging, or regionally important

**Sapienza-adjacent and other Italian groups**: CNR-IREA Naples (Scarfì, Zeni, Sannino) is an experimental-bioeffects group rather than computational dosimetry, but provides rigorous European bioassay validation. Fondazione Ugo Bordoni (Carciofi) runs the Italian national EMF monitoring network. CNR-IEIIT-Torino and Politecnico di Torino's Luca Giaccone chair LF-dosimetry work in ICNIRP.

**Other French groups**: XLIM Limoges (Philippe Lévêque, Delia Arnaud-Cormos) is France's principal experimental-dosimetry group for in-vitro/in-vivo 5G exposure-setup characterization. IMS Bordeaux (Yann Percherancier, Isabelle Lagroye — ICNIRP member) collaborates closely. IETR's mmWave work (above) is the heavyweight computational contribution.

**Other Asian groups**: NITech-Wang (separate from Hirata) does reverberation-chamber rat/human SAR; Chiba University's Jose Gomez-Tames leads personalized brain-stimulation dosimetry; Tokyo Metropolitan University (Masao Taki, emeritus) co-developed TARO; CRIEPI's **Kenichi Yamazaki** sits on the IEEE ICES SC6 Advisory Board; UEC Tokyo's Kun Li chairs SC6 TG6 on APD averaging; KAIST (Seungyoung Ahn) leads WPT exposure dosimetry. In China, the **only mainland-China-affiliated researcher with senior IEEE ICES SC6 leadership is Yinliang Diao at South China Agricultural University**, jointly appointed with NITech. CAICT (Tongning Wu, Congsheng Li) drives Chinese GB 21288-2022 standardization and 5G/mmWave intercomparisons. BUPT, Tsinghua, Southeast University (Wei Hong's antenna group), Xidian, and Shanghai Jiao Tong do strong antenna/mmWave work but not standards-relevant dosimetry.

**UK groups**: UKHSA (Carolina Calderón, Darren Addison, Azadeh Peyman; with Nigel Cridland now on ICNIRP) is the UK regulatory anchor, generating in-situ 5G macro datasets and the tissue dielectric measurements that underpin many models. University of Surrey 5G/6GIC (Ahmed Elzanaty, Rahim Tafazolli) is small but rising as the UK communications-theoretic EMF group, working on aerial RIS for EMF reduction and stochastic geometry of cellular EMF. University of Bristol's Frank de Vocht (ICNIRP) and Imperial's Mireille Toledano (SCAMP, UK COSMOS) are epidemiology-adjacent.

**German/Austrian institutional layer**: BfS Germany hosts the ICNIRP scientific secretariat (Gunde Ziegelberger, Dan Baaken, Blanka Pophof; **Jens Kuhne joined ICNIRP in July 2024**) and funds the German research programme — institutionally critical though not a major publisher of numerical dosimetry. femu (RWTH Aachen, Sarah Drießen) operates **EMF-Portal**, the world's reference EMF-literature database. TU Ilmenau (Matthias Hein, Christian Bornkessel) and RWTH Aachen IHF (Dirk Heberling) recently produced provocative-traffic 5G massive-MIMO measurements (arXiv 2511.17212). IMST GmbH commercializes the **EMPIRE XPU** FDTD simulator. Karolinska Institutet's Maria Feychting is a long-standing ICNIRP member on the epidemiology side.

**Spain**: iTEAM/UPV (Narcís Cardona) and now UPCT (Concepción García Pardo, who **co-chairs the EMF sub-WG of COST CA20120 INTERACT**) lead body-area-network dosimetry and 5G/6G dielectric tissue characterization. ISGlobal Barcelona (Mònica Guxens, Martine Vrijheid, Elisabeth Cardis) coordinates the Horizon Europe **GOLIAT** project — epidemiology-led but with major dose-modeling demand.

**Other European**: Utrecht IRAS (Anke Huss, ICNIRP) and Swiss TPH (Martin Röösli, ICNIRP until 2024) are the dominant epidemiology groups, both heavily reliant on Ghent and IT'IS for the dosimetric component. FORTH Heraklion coordinates the Horizon Europe **NextGEM** project. Sciensano (Belgium), ISSeP (Wallonia), TNO (Netherlands), and Norway's DSA are regulatory rather than research-leading.

**United States (significantly contracted)**: FDA/CDRH remains the only major US center — Wolfgang Kainz (retired 2024, now HPC for MRI Safety LLC) was the central figure for MRI SAR safety and Virtual-Family collaborations with IT'IS; FDA continues with Brian Beard, Leonardo Angelone, Sunder Rajan, Mario Iacono. Air Force Research Laboratory's 711th Human Performance Wing in San Antonio continues the Brooks legacy with ~$44M directed-energy bioeffects funding, focused on pulsed-RF weapon effects rather than consumer-device SAR. Among universities, **Susan Hagness (Wisconsin–Madison, NAE 2026), Cynthia Furse and the emeritus Om Gandhi (Utah, with junior PI Jamesina Simpson continuing stochastic FDTD), Allen Taflove (Northwestern, emeritus), Kenneth Foster (Penn, emeritus, frequent ICES SC6 co-author), Quirino Balzano (Maryland, emeritus, ex-Motorola), and Gianluca Lazzi (USC, neural prosthetics)** are the recognized names — but most are emeritus or pivoted to biomedical imaging. Motorola Solutions' **Antonio Faraone** is the most active US-based industrial dosimetry voice and a long-standing IEEE SC34 leader. C-K Chou (retired Motorola) still chairs IEEE ICES TC95.

**Canada**: Health Canada (Art Thansandote retired, Eric Lemay, James McNamee, Greg Gajda) maintains Safety Code 6 with limited published computational work. McGill (Milica Popović, CompEM) does microwave breast-imaging SAR. U Toronto (Costas Sarris) does FDTD/EMC. Western Univ./Lawson (**Alexandre Legros** co-chairs IEEE ICES SC3).

**Latin America**: Fragmented. UFRN (Marcio Rodrigues et al.) currently appears to be the most active Brazilian group for shared-site BS exposure assessment; CETUC PUC-Rio and INATEL focus on telecom; ANATEL handles regulation aligned with ICNIRP 2020. No internationally visible Brazilian/Mexican/Argentine/Chilean numerical dosimetry program.

## The ML, Sionna RT, and digital-twin frontier

This is a **fast-emerging field originating from wireless-networks groups, not traditional dosimetry labs** — and the two communities are only weakly integrated except via Wiart (Télécom Paris) and Parazzini (CNR-IEIIT). The key actors:

**NVIDIA Research — Jakob Hoydis, Fayçal Aït-Aoudia, Sebastian Cammerer, Merlin Nimier-David** built **Sionna RT** (v0.14 in 2023, v1.0 in 2025; arXiv 2504.21719), the first **differentiable ray-tracer for radio propagation**, built on Mitsuba 3 + Dr.Jit. It is now the substrate for most academic ray-tracing-driven digital-twin work, though NVIDIA itself does not work on EMF safety. **INSA Lyon / Inria CITI — Mohammed Mallik and Guillaume Villemaud** are the most explicitly EMF-focused ML group: **ExposNet** (arXiv 2503.02966, 2025) for deep-learning E-field prediction, **GLIP** (PIMRC 2024) for exposure-map completion by deep generative networks, infinite-width-CNN urban EMF reconstruction (*IEEE Access* 12:49476, 2024), and **deep-reinforcement-learning base-station deployment under EMF constraints** (arXiv 2601.02385, 2026). They collaborate with Wiart, Davy Gaillot (IEMN Lille), and Laurent Clavier (IMT Nord Europe, COST INTERACT chair). **Northeastern WIoT — Tommaso Melodia, Michele Polese, Pedram Johari** built **BostonTwin** (MMSys 2024) and ray-tracing-driven ns-3 cellular simulation with NIST; mainly 6G propagation, with EMF not yet a primary metric but the platform directly applicable. **EURECOM (Florian Kaltenberger)** integrated OpenAirInterface with Sionna RT. **TU Dresden (Fettweis 6G-life)**, **Politecnico di Milano (Spagnolini)**, and **NYU Wireless (Rappaport, Chafii)** are Sionna users without explicit EMF focus. The Tor Vergata–Surrey axis (Chiaraviglio, Bartoletti, Elzanaty) bridges 5G measurements, stochastic geometry, and increasingly ray-tracing-based EMF analyses. From the dosimetry side proper, **Hirata's group (Rashed's deep-learning head models — ForkNet, SHARM, CondNet) is the leading ML-for-dosimetry team globally**, and Wiart's C2M is the leader in stochastic-surrogate models.

## Industry-academic and standards landscape in one snapshot

A handful of industry groups punch far above their headcount because they drove the actual standards. **Ericsson Research's Christer Törnevik, Davide Colombi, and Bo Xu** authored the actual-maximum-power-reduction-factor methodology now embedded in IEC 62232 Ed. 3/4 (Xu et al. *Front. Public Health* 9:777759, 2022) — arguably the single most consequential recent industrial contribution to 5G base-station compliance. Their long-running collaboration with Ghent on commercial-network in-situ massive-MIMO measurements (Aerts et al. *Applied Sciences* 11:3592, 2021) is now the template followed worldwide. Nokia (Christophe Grangeat) and Motorola Solutions (Faraone) anchor the rest of the IEC 62232/62209 project-leader pool.

## Honest tiered ranking of the top groups

For pure **(a) numerical/computational dosimetry** of base stations and UE, the global top ten in roughly descending importance: **IT'IS (Kuster) → NITech (Hirata) → NICT (Watanabe/Wake/Sasaki) → WAVES Ghent (Joseph) → Chaire C2M Télécom Paris (Wiart) → CNR-IEIIT Milan (Parazzini) → IETR Rennes (Zhadobov) → Aalto (Laakso) → Split (Poljak) → Sapienza Rome (Liberti/Apollonio)**. Below this, INRIM Turin, Seibersdorf, ETRI Korea, SCAU-Diao, UEC Tokyo (Li), CRIEPI (Yamazaki), CAICT (Wu/Li), and FDA/CDRH form a strong specialty layer.

For **(c) ML/digital-twin/Sionna-style computational exposure modeling**: **Chaire C2M (Wiart) and NITech (Hirata–Rashed–Kodera) lead the dosimetry-native side**; **INSA Lyon/Inria CITI (Mallik/Villemaud), Northeastern WIoT (Melodia), Tor Vergata (Chiaraviglio), Surrey 5G/6GIC (Elzanaty), and NVIDIA Research (Hoydis)** lead the networks-native side. **CNR-IEIIT Milan and WAVES Ghent are the principal bridges**; SCAU-Diao is the rising Chinese node.

For **(b) measurement-based exposure assessment**: WAVES Ghent and Ericsson Research (Törnevik/Colombi) define the in-situ 5G NR methodology; NICT, ARPANSA/Swinburne, Seibersdorf, UKHSA, and Fondazione Ugo Bordoni are the national champions. SPEAG (IT'IS commercial arm) builds the DASY8 hardware.

For **(d) epidemiology** (adjacent, flagged): **Swiss TPH (Röösli), Utrecht IRAS (Huss), ISGlobal (Guxens/Vrijheid/Cardis), Imperial (Toledano), Bristol (de Vocht), ACEBR (Karipidis/Loughran), Karolinska (Feychting), and BfS** form the WHO systematic-review backbone — they generate the demand for accurate dose models from the groups above.

## What this concentration means

A striking and consequential pattern: **roughly 30 individuals occupy overlapping seats across ICNIRP, IEEE ICES SC6, IEC TC106, and the WHO RF systematic-review program** — Hirata, Karipidis, Joseph, Wiart, Kuster, Watanabe, Sasaki, Laakso, Poljak, Yamazaki, Diao, Colombi/Törnevik appear simultaneously in multiple bodies. This concentration is the basis both of the field's coherent recent output (ICNIRP 2020, IEEE C95.1-2019, IEC 62232 Ed. 3/4, the 2026 personalized-dosimetry roadmap) and of the principal external critique (ICBE-EMF, Hardell–Nilsson, Nyberg et al. on self-referencing authorship behind ICNIRP 2020). Geographically, **leadership has migrated decisively from the US to Japan, Switzerland, Belgium, France, Italy, Australia, and increasingly China and Korea** over the past 15 years — a shift visible in subcommittee chair affiliations, voxel-model provenance, and review-paper first-authorship. The most important strategic implication for anyone tracking this field is that **the next decade's contested ground is the ML/digital-twin layer**, where the dosimetry community (C2M, IEIIT, Hirata–Rashed) and the wireless-networks community (NVIDIA Sionna, INSA Lyon, Northeastern, Tor Vergata, Surrey) are only beginning to merge — and the groups that successfully bridge the two will set the methodology for 6G compliance assessment.
