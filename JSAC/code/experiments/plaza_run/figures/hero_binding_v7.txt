Binding-regime hero for §VII.D, $M = 256$ panel-size sensitivity. Plaza scenario at $43\,$dBm conducted transmit power on the $16\times 16$ panel (256 elements, half-wavelength spacing), $E_{\mathrm{RL}} = 6\,$V/m post-2014 Brussels arr\^et\'e / Italy outdoor attention level (per-body $L_{\mathrm{RL}} = 27.7\,$mW), 9000-slot 5-min realistic-walk trace. (a) ECDFs of $P_{\mathrm{abs}}/L_{\mathrm{RL}}$ across body-slots: MRT exceeds the budget on $39.5\%$ of slots (median $0.72 L_{\mathrm{RL}}$, $99^{\mathrm{th}}$ percentile $4.23 L_{\mathrm{RL}}$, more than double the $M = 64$ median), WC back-off scales MRT down to $0.19 L_{\mathrm{RL}}$ median ($2.0\%$ violations, identical to $M = 64$ since the same min-margin rule applies), ZF moves to $0.19 L_{\mathrm{RL}}$ median but with $18.7\%$ body-slot violations and $99^{\mathrm{th}}$ percentile $3.73 L_{\mathrm{RL}}$, the proposed multi-body ECBF returns the min-absorption fallback on $88.5\%$ of slots (the higher dimensionality widens the $\bm\lambda$ dynamic range that the $\textsf{max\_outer}=8$ Newton cap of \cref{sec:multibody-ecbf} handles), and the per-body oracle holds at the budget with $71.3\%$ fallback. (b) Per-precoder violation rate vs.\ mean sum-rate. MRT and WC back-off split compliance at the same $24.5\,$Gbps SE cap, ZF reaches $50.4\,$Gbps but lands at $(18.7\%, 50.4\,\text{Gbps})$ outside the compliance corner, the proposed ECBF and oracle yield $3.7$ and $11.0\,$Gbps respectively. The $M = 256$ takeaway is that ZF compliance is no longer free: with $231$ nulling DOFs over $K = 25$ served users the array still produces high-gain ridges that bodies cross, and the geometric solution is increasingly non-compliant as the array grows. The proposed solver's value emerges most cleanly at high $M$ where the budget-aware constraint cannot be replaced by any geometric proxy.
