### Issues discovered

1. **Fresnel back-facing inconsistency**: Incoherent kernels clip mu to [0,1] for Fresnel (so back-facing gets T(mu=0) = nonzero), while coherent kernels zero out the Fresnel operator for back-facing paths. The incoherent path should probably zero out Fresnel for mu <= 0 too, but the ReLU/GELU activation already gates the final Sab to ~0, so the impact is minimal.

2. **Cache growth**: `_G_cache` (now with content hash) and `_fibonacci_sphere_cache` grow unboundedly. For long-running viewer sessions with many body switches, this could consume memory. Consider adding an LRU eviction policy.
