Chat summary of the issue:
It’s pushing a shift from “digital twins of wireless networks” to “wireless networks enabling digital twin networks (DTNs)”: DTs act as real-time agents that sense the physical world, run predictions/optimization, and close the loop by reconfiguring RAN/edge/cloud resources to meet application/mission goals (QoE, latency, reliability, energy, resilience), not just network KPIs.

Strongest ideas in the scope

Twin-in-the-loop control: DTs directly inside the feedback loop (sense → predict → act → verify).

Cross-layer optimization: jointly tune wireless + edge/cloud + scheduling/dataflow for mission objectives.

Learning-based control: RL / differentiable control with stability + safety guarantees.

Predictive resilience: DT analytics for proactive fault detection, self-healing, robustness.

Scalable orchestration: distributed/federated DTs across edge–cloud; dynamic creation/migration.

Semantic / task-aware networking: optimize “meaning” (task/QoE) rather than packets; intent-based APIs.

Trustworthiness: latency-bounded synchronization, security/privacy, robust data pipelines, interoperability/standards.

Strongest way to fit exposure into this (your angle)

Make exposure a first-class control objective/constraint in the closed loop (SAR/APD risk budgets), enabled by a fast exposure micro-twin surrogate trained from Sim4Life/FDTD over phantoms/poses/device placements. Online DTN control uses it to adapt beams/power/scheduling safely under uncertainty.
