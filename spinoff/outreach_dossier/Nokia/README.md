## Purpose of this dossier

Threefold:

- **Sound like a peer.** Grangeat has done this tutorial 40 times. If Robin
  fumbles the difference between "product compliance" (Clause 6.1) and "product
  installation compliance" (Clause 6.2), or does not know what F_PR is, or asks
  something already answered in the case-study table on slide 7 of his own deck,
  the call is over. Every technical claim in this dossier is calibrated to that
  fluency bar.
- **Ask the questions that are not already knowable.** Grangeat has published
  extensively on beamforming impact, per-beam power distributions, moving-UE
  effect, and MAC-layer control. `05_open_questions.md` is the list of things
  he can answer that Robin cannot Google.
- **Not lead on differentiability, not drop it either.** Filip's guardrail is
  the three-sentence description, differentiability included. The
  July-2026 13-agent study concluded differentiability is not a business moat
  but named surviving true claims. Neither position licences dropping the word,
  only reweighting the emphasis. Robin's own read: differentiability is not
  dead. See `04_call_prep.md` for the exact framing.

## What this dossier is not

- A pitch deck. Nothing here is for external eyes.
- A validation of the method. AEGIS's technical merits are established elsewhere.
- A CRM entry. That is `contact_sheet.md`.
- A licence proposal. Stage-2 discovery call, no NDA, no document attached.
