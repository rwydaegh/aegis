% PREV: The calculation considered here requires the established parts in one declared
% PREV: model. Registered panoramas must refer to the same city geometry that supports
% PREV: the propagation calculation. A source model must remain fixed across sites, and
% PREV: its physical scale must be separate from the numerical source quadrature.
% PREV: Direct, specular, and diffuse power must remain separate until their arrival
% PREV: directions are coupled to the body. The method in this paper meets these
% PREV: requirements with a panorama-derived material surface, a route-aligned roofline
% PREV: source measure, and a transport model that stops after the first diffuse material
% PREV: interaction. This combination defines a conditional comparison of local scenes
% PREV: without treating the available standpoints as a population sample or the
% PREV: roofline sources as a measured deployment. To the best of the authors'
% PREV: knowledge, prior work has not combined registered street panoramas, a common roofline source law, component-resolved
% PREV: first-material transport, and directional body coupling along fixed routes.
% NEXT: The contributions are as follows.
% NEXT: \begin{enumerate}
% NEXT:   \item A scene-construction method binds registered panorama
% NEXT:   evidence to the transport mesh. Every image-derived assignment retains its
% NEXT:   provenance, and unobserved or refused regions retain a declared geometric
% NEXT:   fallback.
% NEXT:
% NEXT:   \item A normalized roofline source model and transport calculation preserve
% NEXT:   direct, one-reflection specular, and first-diffuse power and
% NEXT:   direction until whole-body coupling.
% NEXT:
% NEXT:   \item A five-site application reports fixed-route exposure distributions,
% NEXT:   controlled first-diffuse validation, component closure, replica convergence,
% NEXT:   and the retained transport at the six standpoints with no direct or
% NEXT:   one-reflection specular contribution.
% NEXT: \end{enumerate}
The study applies this method at 15~GHz to five selected routes with 73 fixed
standpoints. Each result is normalized per unit active-source areal density and
per unit equivalent isotropically radiated power. The retained transport contains
the exact direct term, exact one-reflection specular term, and the first diffuse
interaction evaluated by next-event estimation. It contains no higher specular
order and no path continuation after a diffuse interaction. The reported route
distributions are therefore results for five fixed routes under this source and
transport model. They are not estimates of population exposure, deployed-network
exposure, or a ranking of the five cities.
