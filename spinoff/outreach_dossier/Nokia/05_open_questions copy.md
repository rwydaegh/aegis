# What we still don't know — the un-Googleable questions

These are the questions I could not close with public sources during the
research pass. That is exactly why they are worth asking Grangeat directly.
Every answer here is quotable IOF gold because it is genuinely non-obvious.

## Standards / committee

- **Is TR 62669 Ed. 3.0 published yet, or has the timeline slipped past mid-2025?**
  Tutorial slide 30 says "planned publication 2025." Publicly, only the 2019
  Ed. 2.0 is on the IEC webstore as of my search. If Ed. 3.0 has slipped, that
  matters for when Robin's contribution window opens.
- **How many of the 30+ Ed. 3.0 case studies came from Nokia?**
  Nokia's Poland cluster has published enough to seed several. If the answer
  is "5 of 30 are ours," that says a lot about who dominates the committee.
- **Who else in MT3 is receptive to computational-method innovation?**
  The convenor cannot table a contribution on his own. Who are the natural
  seconders inside MT3 for a new class of computational method? Grangeat
  will know without hesitation.
- **Is there active work in MT3 or ICES SC4 on convergence between IEC 62232
  (base station) and IEC/IEEE 63195-2 (device APD)?**
  Some countries run aggregate-exposure assessments combining both. If MT3
  and SC4 are cross-pollinating, that's the natural home for a method like
  AEGIS that spans both.

- **How does MT3 think about acceptance criteria for physics-informed ML
  surrogate methods?**
  Bilson NPL et al. R² = 0.86. Where does the committee draw the line for

  differentiable-EM methods. Same infrastructure.
