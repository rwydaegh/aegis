Fig.~\ref{fig:route-distributions} shows the fixed-route empirical distributions.
Table~\ref{tab:route-results} gives the central
summaries and finite multipath surplus. Normalized whole-body SAR is reported in
m$^2$~kg$^{-1}$ per unit $\rho_A P_{\mathrm{EIRP}}$. A physical whole-body SAR
value therefore requires multiplication by the deployment's areal source density
and EIRP.
The figure includes all 163 observation points and marks the six with zero direct
and zero order-1 specular transfer. The largest route median (Mexico City,
0.1291~m$^2$~kg$^{-1}$) is 14.31 times the smallest (Milan,
0.009016~m$^2$~kg$^{-1}$). Mexico City and Tokyo Hachiko have the widest spread
along a route and the lowest tails.
